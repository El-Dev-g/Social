# Zooz Business Portal

Welcome to the **Zooz Business Platform**. Designed for advertisers, commerce merchants, and enterprise brands building professional pages on the Zooz social ecosystem.

## Offerings
- **Ad Manager**: Configure campaigns, target demographics, and monitor ROI.
- **Enterprise Profiles**: Set up custom business headers, interactive action buttons, and automated FAQ responses.
- **Analytics & Webhooks**: Real-time webhook configuration for external CRM integrations.
- **Verification Support**: Standard business verification steps and badge application templates.
