# Zooz Artist Hub

Welcome to the **Zooz Artist Hub**. Designed similarly to professional dashboard ecosystems (like Spotify for Artists or Meta Creator Studio for Music), this space is dedicated to musicians, sound designers, and independent record labels publishing to the Zooz audio library.

## Core Pillars
- **Profile Verification**: Seamless application for the verified blue music badge on your artist bio page.
- **Analytics & Reach**: Real-time tracking of post associations, video backgrounds using your music tracks, and cumulative stream metrics.
- **Visual Visualizer Integrations**: Canvas / looping background video upload structures for active streams in stories and short videos.
- **Concert & Event Ticketing**: Embedded ticket sales integrations and merchandise linking on artist feed posts.

## Getting Started
To verify your artist profile:
1. Contact support or upload your standard DDEX catalog reference.
2. Link your official social login credentials to prove identity.
3. Configure your profile banners, custom bio, and stream overlays.
